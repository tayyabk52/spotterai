01Personal Scheduling

Set your hours. Protect your home time.

Define when you are ready to roll and lock in planned return windows before picking up your next run. The app synchronizes dispatch offers with your personal calendar.

  1. Ready-Now Status

    Signal your availability to the network with exact readiness dates, equipment readiness, and preferred departure times.

  2. Guaranteed Home Time

    Lock in return windows so the dispatch algorithm only recommends return routes that bring you home on schedule.

  3. Zero Forced Scheduling

    Eliminate unwanted layovers and unexpected dispatch gaps with predictive calendar visibility.

02Destination Defense

Blacklist bad markets. Protect your rate per mile.

Select the destination markets you want to run to and avoid unprofitable freight deserts. The interactive NO-GO map ensures you never get trapped in deadhead dead zones.

  1. Interactive NO-GO Map

    Highlight regions and metros you decline to enter. The algorithm automatically filters out loads terminating in those territories.

  2. Destination Targeting

    Specify high-volume freight corridors with reliable outbound load volume to maximize your weekly revenue per mile.

  3. Deadhead Reduction

    Evaluate every destination by its backhaul viability before you commit to rolling.

03Machine Matching

Highest-score recommendations delivered first.

Spotter evaluates freight opportunities across thousands of market signals. Loads are scored and ranked in real time by net revenue yield, deadhead miles, and delivery certainty.

  1. Multi-Factor Load Score

    Every offer receives a composite score reflecting rate per mile, deadhead distance, fuel index, and lane turnaround speed.

  2. Ranked Recommendation Feed

    Skip endless load-board scrolling. Review tailored recommendations pre-sorted by highest operational profitability.

  3. Transparent Rate Cards

    Review gross pay, estimated fuel expenditure, and net take-home before accepting or declining.
